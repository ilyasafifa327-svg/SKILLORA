Frequently Asked Questions

A few things to know

Here are some answers to common questions about this demonstration.

Is SKILLORA a real learning platform?

SKILLORA is a front-end demonstration of a learning platform. The courses and learning paths shown on the website are sample content.

Can I enrol in a course through this website?

The website demonstrates course discovery and learning journeys. It does not provide real enrolment, lessons, or accredited certificates.

Does the contact form send a real email?

No. This version checks your form using JavaScript and displays a demonstration message. It does not send emails or store messages on a server.

Does SKILLORA require an account?

No real account is required for this demonstration. The website does not collect account passwords or process payments.
